The `slugify` function has been added to `shop/utils.py`. It:

1. Converts the text to lowercase
2. Replaces every run of non-alphanumeric characters with a single '-'
3. Strips leading and trailing '-'

Example usage:
- `slugify("Hello World!")` → `"hello-world"`
- `slugify("  ---Test---  ")` → `"test"`
- `slugify("Café")` → `"café"` (keeps the accented character)
